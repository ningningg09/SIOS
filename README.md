# Survive in Outer Space!

A beginner-friendly HTML, CSS, and JavaScript space survival game where you manage the resources of a lunar or Martian outpost and make decisions to keep the crew alive.

## About the Project

In **Survive in Outer Space!** you are responsible for managing a space outpost with limited resources.

Each day, the outpost's resources decrease automatically, and unexpected events force you to make difficult decisions.

You must manage:

- Oxygen
- Food
- Power
- Radiation Shielding

If any resource reaches **0%**, the mission fails.

Your goal is to survive the entire mission.

## Lunar vs. Martian Outpost

The game currently has two environments:

### Lunar Outpost

The Moon has limited resources and a harsh environment.

Players encounter problems such as:

- Food contamination
- Lunar dust covering solar panels
- Air leaks
- Equipment failures
- Radiation shielding malfunctions

### Martian Outpost

Mars is designed to be more difficult, with more dangerous events.

Players may encounter:

- Food contamination
- Martian dust storms
- Habitat leaks
- Major equipment failures
- Equipment failures

## How to Play

1. Choose either the **Lunar Outpost** or **Martian Outpost**.
2. Start your mission with all resources at 100%.
3. Each day, your resources decrease automatically.
4. Random events may occur.
5. If an event requires a decision, choose how to respond.
6. Different choices consume different resources.
7. Keep every resource above 0%.
8. Survive all 15 days to complete the mission.
